Size guide

How big each format is, so you know what's arriving and where it'll live.

Sizes and weights of each record format
Format Packaging Disc or tape Weight Shelve it on
Vinyl LP 12-inch 31.5 × 31.5 cm sleeve, about 0.5 cm thick (1 cm for a double LP) 30 cm disc 250–350 g An LP shelf at least 33 cm tall
Vinyl single 7-inch 18 × 18 cm sleeve 17.5 cm disc 60–80 g A 7-inch box or a bookshelf
CD 14.2 × 12.5 × 1 cm case 12 cm disc About 100 g A CD rack or a bookshelf
Cassette 10.9 × 7 × 1.7 cm case 10 × 6.3 cm tape About 50 g A cassette rack or a drawer
Boxset Varies — CD boxsets are usually around 15 × 15 × 5 cm Several discs, plus extras 0.5–1 kg Lying flat, or standing on an LP shelf

Standard sizes. Special packaging, like gatefolds and boxsets, can run a little bigger.
