Quick answers

Most questions are answered here — the form's just below if yours isn't.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 2–3 business days in Metro Manila, 3–5 in the rest of Luzon, 4–6 in the Visayas and 5–7 in Mindanao. Express is next business day in Metro Manila and 1–3 days everywhere else.

How much is shipping?

Standard shipping is ₱99 in Metro Manila and Luzon, ₱180 to the Visayas and ₱200 to Mindanao. Express costs ₱199–₱390. Checkout shows the exact price once you pick your region.

Where's my order?

Sign in and open My orders — each order shows whether it's being packed, on its way or delivered.

A record arrived warped or bent. What now?

Tell us within 30 days and we'll replace it or refund you, postage on us. Pick the order in the form below so we know which one.

The size guide lists every format's size and weight.

How can I pay?
