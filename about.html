About

A small room, a lot of cardboard, and a fairly serious opinion about sleeve corners.

Two sides of the same record

Vendio started the way most record shops start — with one crate too many and nowhere sensible to put it. What began as a shelf of duplicates became a room, then a second room, then a small operation that ships sleeves to people who care whether the corners arrive square.

Every record listed on this site has been through the same short ritual: unpacked, checked against the pressing notes, photographed on the same square of grey card, and sleeved again before it goes back in the crate. The photographs you see on the Browse page are the actual copies, not stock art from a distributor.

We press nothing ourselves. We just find the good copies, describe them honestly, and wrap them like they matter — because the difference between a record that survives the post and one that doesn't is usually about four pence worth of stiffened card.

Customer support

The counter is staffed Monday to Friday, 10am–6pm. Anything ordered after 4pm goes out the next working day.

Quote your order reference when you write in — it's at the top of your confirmation email, in the form VND-<year>-<number>.

If a sleeve turns up with a bent corner or a disc arrives warped, tell us within 30 days and we'll replace it or refund you — postage on us. Get in touch.
