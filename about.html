Since the first crate

About

A small room, a lot of cardboard, and a fairly serious opinion about sleeve corners. Who packs these?

Two sides of the same record

VenAudio started the way most record shops start — with one crate too many and nowhere sensible to put it. What began as a shelf of duplicates became a room, then a second room, then a small operation that ships sleeves to people who care whether the corners arrive square.

Every record listed on this site has been through the same short ritual: unpacked, checked against the pressing notes, photographed on the same square of grey card, and sleeved again before it goes back in the crate. The photographs you see on the Browse page are the actual copies, not stock art from a distributor.

We press nothing ourselves. We just find the good copies, describe them honestly, and wrap them like they matter — because the difference between a record that survives the post and one that doesn't is usually a few pesos’ worth of stiffened card.

How we pack

Every order, every time — it's why the corners arrive square.

  1. Checked

    Unpacked and checked against the pressing notes — no warps, no scuffs.

  2. Sleeved

    A fresh inner sleeve, and a clear outer one to keep the cover clean.

  3. Boxed

    Stiff card on both sides and a snug box, so nothing slides in the post.
